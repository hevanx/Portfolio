Case Study: Organic Instagram Reels Growth for an Independent Label

Short-form content system from scratch

Overview

Platform: Instagram Reels
Timeframe: 90 days
Client: Krok Records (Independent Music Label)
My role: Social Media Coordinator — strategy, scripting, hook writing, filming, editing, captions, publishing

Starting Point

The account began with a small audience and needed consistent reach, profile actions, and follower growth. Grew to 847 followers — a 600% increase — with 726 net new followers in 90 days.

Strategy

  • Built a repeatable short-form format designed for retention and shares.
  • Optimized hooks to communicate the point in the first 1–2 seconds.
  • Kept posting consistent and sustainable to protect quality and avoid burnout.
  • Made content in-house end-to-end to iterate quickly without bottlenecks.

Execution

  • Scripted and wrote on-screen hooks.
  • Edited for pacing and clarity.
  • Appeared on camera to build trust and improve conversion from views to follows.

Results

  • 590,000+ total views across Reels, including top-performing content exceeding 100K views.
  • Reached 227,000+ accounts in the last 90 days, with 94% of views from non-followers.
  • 63,000+ total interactions through retention-focused content.
  • Grew account to 847 followers (+600%), with 726 net new followers in 90 days.
  • Increased profile activity by 413%, including 3,000+ profile visits and increased external link clicks.

What I Learned

  • Low-volume posting can outperform high-volume output when the hook and pacing are strong.
  • On-camera content improves trust and follow-through when the message is clear.
  • Consistency makes testing easier and results more reliable.

About

I currently handle social media for Krok Records, an independent music label, where I manage strategy, content production, and publishing end-to-end. I'm drawn to work where the feedback loop is fast and the results are measurable — short-form content fits that perfectly.

I handle everything in-house — scripting, filming, editing, and publishing — which means I'm never waiting on anyone to ship, test, or adjust. When something isn't working, I can identify it in the data and change it in the next post.
